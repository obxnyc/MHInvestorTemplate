THIS PURCHASE AGREEMENT (“Agreement”), made this the 27th day of July, 2026, by and between Habberstad Norse Ventures LLC, as Purchaser, and Larabee Homes LLC, as Seller.
W I T N E S S E T H
That Seller has offered to sell that certain mobile home to wit:
Champion Legend Series 14x66 3bd/2ba Serial# to be furnished upon CO

The total purchase price for the mobile home is $95,600.00 (the “Purchase Price”), payable as follows:
(a)	$33,460.00 upon execution of this Agreement as a down payment, receipt of which is acknowledged by Seller; and
(b)	the balance of $62,140.00, together with interest at the rate of 5.20% per annum, shall be paid in three hundred sixty (360) consecutive monthly installments of $341.22 each, commencing on _________, 2026 and continuing on the _____ day of each succeeding month until paid in full. The entire unpaid principal balance, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and other sums due hereunder, shall in all events be due and payable on _________, 20____  (the “Maturity Date”). This loan is amortized over a thirty (30) year term in accordance with the amortization schedule attached hereto as Schedule A.  All payments shall first be applied to accrued interest, then to principal, and thereafter to any other amounts properly due hereunder. Seller shall provide Purchaser, upon reasonable request, with a written statement showing the outstanding principal balance and the application of payments previously made. Payments shall be made to Seller at the address set forth above, or at such other place as Seller may designate in writing.  Purchaser shall have the absolute right to prepay all or any portion of the outstanding balance at any time without premium, penalty or other charge.  Any partial prepayment shall be applied to reduce principal.
Late Payment & Default. Any installment not received within 10 days of its due date is subject to a late charge of $100. If Purchaser shall default in the payment of any installment and such default shall continue for a period of thirty (30) days after delivery of written notice from Seller, then Seller may, declare the entire unpaid balance immediately due and payable, and Seller shall be entitled to pursue all remedies available at law, including repossession of the mobile home described above.
Security Interest. Until the purchase price is paid in full, Seller retains a purchase-money security interest in and lien upon the mobile home described above. Title and/or the certificate of title shall remain with Seller and shall not pass to Purchaser until all sums due hereunder are paid in full. Purchaser shall not sell, transfer, remove, or further encumber the mobile home without Seller's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.
Insurance & Taxes. Purchaser shall keep the mobile home insured against fire and casualty in an amount equal to the unpaid balance due Seller or the full insurable value of the mobile home, whichever is less, naming Seller as loss payee, and shall provide proof upon request. Purchaser shall be responsible for all personal-property taxes assessed on the Mobile home commencing on delivery of the mobile home and thereafter during the term of this Agreement.
That the parties agree that if this purchase shall not be completed as contracted due to breach by the Seller, then all funds previously paid hereunder by Purchaser shall be refunded to Purchaser. The parties further agree that if this purchase shall not be completed as contracted due to breach by Purchaser, then all funds previously paid hereunder by Purchaser shall be forfeited to Seller as liquidated damages resulting from such breach.
Upon payment in full of all amounts due hereunder, Seller shall execute and deliver such documents as are reasonably necessary to release Seller's lien and transfer or confirm clear title to Purchaser, including a fully executed and notarized bill of sale completely detailing the mobile home being purchased, free and clear of all encumbrances, debts, liens, claims.
Seller represents that Seller is the lawful owner of the mobile home and has authority to sell it. At the time required for transfer of title, Seller shall convey title free of liens created by Seller, other than Seller's lien securing Purchaser's obligations under this Agreement.  Seller further represents to Purchaser that Seller has no knowledge of any legal impediment to the sale of the mobile home described above. If ownership of the mobile home is transferred by Certificate of Title, then the transfer of the same to Purchaser, or as directed by Purchaser, shall serve the same purpose as the Bill of Sale and shall be accepted by Purchaser in lieu thereof.

Notices. Any notice required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses stated above, or to such other address as a party may designate by written notice.  Any additional method or period of notice required by applicable law shall control.
Counterparts; Electronic Signatures
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall constitute an original and all of which together shall constitute one agreement. Electronic and PDF signatures shall be deemed originals and shall be binding upon the parties to the fullest extent permitted by law.
