MOBILE HOME PARK LAND LEASE AGREEMENT
This Mobile Home Park Land Lease Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the 1st day of September, 2026, by and between: Park Owner: Benjamin Cade Larabee (“Park Owner”); Mobile Home Owner: Habberstad Norse Ventures LLC (“Homeowner”); Lot Number: 3100 Lady Viola Dr (“Lot”).
1. Premises
Park Owner agrees to lease to Homeowner, and Homeowner agrees to lease from Park Owner, the parcel of land identified as Lot 3100 Lady Viola Dr in The Retreat at Cross Creek (the “Park”). This lease entitles the Homeowner to place and maintain a mobile home on the designated Lot within the Park. The Lot consists solely of the land comprising the designated home site, together with Homeowner’s right to use such common areas, roads and facilities of the Park as Park Owner may from time to time designate for the common use of Park residents, subject to this Lease and the rules and regulations of the Park.
2. Term
This Agreement shall be effective as of the date above and shall continue in effect on a month-to-month basis, unless terminated by either party in accordance with the terms herein. Notwithstanding the month-to-month nature of this Agreement, Park Owner shall not voluntarily terminate this Agreement while Homeowner is current on any existing debt owed on the mobile home, except for cause and upon not less than sixty (60) days’ prior written notice to Homeowner, or such longer period as may be required by applicable law. Notwithstanding the foregoing, in the event Park Owner sells, transfers, or otherwise disposes of the Park, or ceases to operate the Park as a mobile home community, Park Owner or its successor may terminate this Agreement upon not less than one hundred eighty (180) days’ prior written notice to Homeowner, whether or not Homeowner is then current on any debt owed on the mobile home. If such termination occurs while Homeowner still owes a balance to Park Owner or its affiliates, Homeowner may, at its option, prepay the remaining balance without penalty or relocate the mobile home, and Park Owner shall cooperate reasonably in any such relocation or payoff.
3. Lot Rent
Homeowner agrees to pay to Park Owner monthly rent in the amount of $375.00 (Three Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars) for the use of the Lot. This amount is subject to change as specified below.
4. Rent Adjustment
The monthly lot rent is $375.00. Any increase in rent shall be effective 60 days following written notice from Park Owner to Homeowner; provided, however, in no event shall the lot rent increase to an amount greater than the lot rent then being paid by at least thirty-three percent (33%) of the homeowners in the Park.
5. Monthly Consulting Fee
In addition to the Lot Rent specified in Section 3, the Homeowner agrees to pay a monthly consulting fee of $45.00 (Forty-Five Dollars) to Park Owner (or an affiliate thereof) for ongoing consulting services. This fee will remain in effect for the duration of this Agreement, provided that the Homeowner or its affiliates maintains an interest in the mobile home park land owned by Benjamin Cade Larabee or any of its affiliates. The Park Owner may adjust or terminate the consulting fee upon 60 days’ written notice to the Homeowner; provided, however, in no event shall the consulting fee increase to an amount greater than the consulting fee then being paid by at least thirty-three percent (33%) of the homeowners in the Park.
6. Park Rules and Regulations
Both the Homeowner and the Park Owner agree to comply with all rules and regulations of the Park, as they may be amended from time to time. These rules and regulations will be provided to the Homeowner upon execution of this Agreement and may be updated with reasonable notice. Homeowner is responsible for ensuring that their guests, tenants, and any other occupants of the mobile home also comply with these rules.
7. Use of Premises
The Homeowner agrees to use the leased Lot exclusively for the placement and maintenance of a mobile home and related uses consistent with the Park’s rules and regulations. The Homeowner shall not use the Lot for any unlawful or hazardous activities.
8. Maintenance and Repairs
The Homeowner is responsible for maintaining the mobile home in a safe and sanitary condition, including all plumbing, electrical, and mechanical systems within the mobile home. The Park Owner shall maintain the common areas of the Park, including roads, utilities, and any shared amenities, unless otherwise agreed.
9. Termination
Subject to the terms set forth in Section 2 hereof, either party may terminate this Agreement by providing 60 days’ written notice to the other party. If the Homeowner wishes to vacate the premises or terminate the lease, the Homeowner must give written notice to the Park Owner at least 60 days prior to vacating. The Park Owner may terminate this Agreement for cause in accordance with applicable laws. Homeowner shall not remove the mobile home from the Lot while any amount is owed to Park Owner or its affiliates without Park Owner’s consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.
10. Indemnification
Homeowner agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Park Owner from any loss, damage, or liability arising out of Homeowner’s use of the Lot or any violation of this Agreement or Park rules and regulations. Park Owner shall indemnify and hold harmless Homeowner and Homeowner’s subtenants from any loss, damage, or liability arising out of the negligence or willful misconduct of Park Owner or Park Owner’s agents, employees, contractors or representatives. Each party shall bear its own costs of defense.
11. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the state of North Carolina, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
12. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes any prior negotiations, understandings, or agreements, whether written or oral, between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof.
13. Right of First Refusal, Sale of Mobile Home, and Renter Approval
(a) Right of First Refusal: In the event the Homeowner wishes to sell the mobile home, the Park Owner shall have the first right of refusal to purchase the mobile home. The Homeowner must provide written notice to the Park Owner of the intent to sell, including the terms of the sale, and the Park Owner shall have fifteen (15) days to respond with an offer to purchase the mobile home on the same terms, and must close within thirty (30) days of such notice by Homeowner. If the Park Owner does not exercise the right of first refusal, or fails to close within said thirty (30) days, then the Homeowner may proceed with the sale to a third party.
(b) Park Owner’s Consent to Sale: Where the mobile home will remain on the Lot, the Park Owner’s consent is required for the sale of the mobile home, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld provided the proposed purchaser meets the qualifications for residency in the Park, including but not limited to the buyer’s ability to comply with the Park’s rules and regulations.
(c) Outstanding Debt on Home: Any sale of the mobile home by the Homeowner is contingent upon the payment in full of any existing debt or financial obligations owed on the mobile home. The Homeowner must ensure that all such debts are satisfied prior to completing the sale.
(d) Renter and Subtenant Approval: Homeowner shall have the right to lease, sublease, license or otherwise permit the occupancy of Homeowner’s mobile home to or by any third party (a “Subtenant”), with Park Owner’s consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, provided that (i) the proposed Subtenant first satisfies the Park Owner’s standard screening criteria, including background, credit and income verification applied on a uniform and non-discriminatory basis, (ii) the Subtenant uses the home for lawful residential purposes, and (iii) the Subtenant agrees in writing to comply with the reasonable and uniformly enforced rules and regulations of the Park. Any sublease of the home shall not constitute an assignment or transfer of this Agreement, and Homeowner shall retain all rights under this Lease, including Homeowner’s rights with respect to the Lot and Homeowner’s ownership of the home.
(e) Reserve Fund: The Homeowner agrees to maintain a reserve fund in the amount of $500.00 (Five Hundred Dollars) for each Lot leased by Homeowner in the Park (up to a maximum of $2,500.00 in the aggregate) to cover any potential expenses that may arise during the term of this Agreement. This reserve must be kept in an accessible account, and the Park Owner may require proof of the reserve fund’s availability upon request.
14. Notices
Any notices required to be given under this Agreement shall be delivered in writing. Notices to the Park Owner must be delivered in writing at least 60 days prior to any proposed action, such as termination, rent adjustment, or other significant changes. Notices to the Homeowner may be delivered by mail, in person, overnight courier, or by electronic means if agreed upon by both parties.
15. Counterparts and Electronic Signatures
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall constitute an original and all of which together shall constitute one agreement. Electronic and PDF signatures shall be deemed originals and shall be binding upon the parties to the fullest extent permitted by law.
